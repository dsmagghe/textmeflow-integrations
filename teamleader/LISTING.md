# Teamleader Marketplace-listing

Naam (alle talen): **TextMeFlow WhatsApp**

## English

**Short:** Send WhatsApp messages automatically from Teamleader Focus: invoices, meetings and won deals.

**Long:** Connect your own WhatsApp number to Teamleader Focus through TextMeFlow. When an invoice is booked, your customer gets a message with the amount, due date and payment link. When a meeting is scheduled, attendees get a confirmation and a reminder the day before. When a deal is won, the contact gets a thank-you message. Messages only go to contacts you tagged "WhatsApp allowed" (tag name configurable). You edit every text per language (nl, en, fr, de) and can switch each event on or off.

**Features**
- Invoice booked: amount, due date, payment link
- Meeting scheduled: confirmation plus reminder the day before
- Deal won: thank-you message
- Opt-in per contact (tag or custom field), nothing is sent without it
- Texts in Dutch, English, French and German, in the contact's language
- Test message and one-click disconnect
- Free up to 50 messages per month

**Screenshots:** 1. Settings page with events switched on. 2. Editing a message text with placeholders. 3. Opt-in tag in a Teamleader contact. 4. A received WhatsApp invoice message.

**FAQ**
- Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account? No, you link your own number by scanning a QR code.
- Who receives messages? Only contacts with your opt-in tag.
- What does it cost? Free up to 50 messages per month; paid plans on textmeflow.eu/pricing.
- How do I disconnect? Settings page, "Disconnect": the connection is unregistered at Teamleader and access is deleted.

**Privacy / external service:** Phone numbers, names and message texts are sent to TextMeFlow (hosted in the EU, Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria) and delivered through WhatsApp. https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy - https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms
