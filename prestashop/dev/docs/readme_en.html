TextMeFlow - WhatsApp order updates

User guide, module version 1.0.0, PrestaShop 8.0 to 9.x

This module sends your customers WhatsApp messages about their orders (payment received, shipped, delivered, or any other order status), from your own WhatsApp number, through the TextMeFlow service. Customers only receive messages when they ticked the WhatsApp opt-in box.

1. Installation

  1. In the back office, open Modules > Module Manager and click Upload a module. Choose textmeflow.zip.
  2. Click Configure when the installation is finished.

2. Connect your TextMeFlow account

  1. Click Connect with TextMeFlow. You are sent to the TextMeFlow website.
  2. Sign in, or create a free account, and click Allow. You come back to the configuration page automatically.

You never copy or paste an API key. The connection key is stored encrypted in your shop and is never shown in the browser.

3. Link your WhatsApp number

After connecting, the configuration page shows a QR code. On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to Settings > Linked devices > Link a device and scan the code, just like WhatsApp Web. The page then shows Connected with WhatsApp number .... A code is valid for about two minutes; a new one appears automatically.

4. Messages

Test message

Enter your own mobile number under Your own mobile number and click Send me a test message.

Order status messages

The table lists every order status of your shop. Tick Send a message for the statuses that should send a WhatsApp message to the customer. By default payment accepted, shipped and delivered are on. Leave the text empty to use the ready-made text in the language of the order (English, Dutch, French or German), or write your own text with these placeholders:

PlaceholderReplaced by
{first_name}First name of the customer
{order_number}Order reference
{order_total}Total paid, with currency
{shop_name}Name of your shop
{tracking_url}Carrier tracking link, or the order history page when there is no tracking number
{status}Name of the new order status

New-order message to yourself

Tick Send me a WhatsApp message for every new order to receive a short message on your own number for each new order. Click Save.

Where to see what was sent

Every customer message adds a private note to the order (Orders > order > Messages): "TextMeFlow: WhatsApp message ... sent to +32...", or "not sent" with the reason. Errors are also written to Advanced Parameters > Logs. A failing message never blocks an order or a status change.

5. Customer consent (opt-in)

When the module is connected, a checkbox "Send me my order updates via WhatsApp (via TextMeFlow). You can withdraw this at any time." appears on the registration form, in the personal information step of the checkout (also for guests) and in My account > Information. It is never ticked by default. Without this consent the customer receives no message. Customers can withdraw their consent in My account > Information. When a customer is deleted, the consent record is deleted too.

The phone number is taken from the delivery address (mobile phone first, then phone), or from the invoice address. Numbers without a country code are converted with the country of the address.

6. Disconnect or uninstall

Click Disconnect at the bottom of the configuration page: the connection is revoked at TextMeFlow and no more messages are sent. Uninstalling the module also revokes the connection and removes all its settings and the consent table.

7. Privacy

To deliver a message, the module sends the recipient's phone number and the message text to TextMeFlow (EU-hosted, operated by Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria). Nothing is sent before you connect your account. You remain responsible for your customers' consent; the module provides the opt-in checkbox and only sends customer messages with that consent. The privacy policy and terms of TextMeFlow are linked on the configuration page.

8. Costs

The module is free. The TextMeFlow service is free up to 50 messages per month; larger volumes need a paid TextMeFlow plan.

9. Requirements

  • PrestaShop 8.0 to 9.x, PHP 7.2 or higher with the cURL extension.
  • The shop must be able to make outgoing HTTPS requests.
  • A phone with WhatsApp to link your number.
