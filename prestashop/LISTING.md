# Listing-tekst voor PrestaShop Addons

Naam (alle talen): **TextMeFlow - WhatsApp order updates**
(Naam begint met "TextMeFlow"; geen WhatsApp- of PrestaShop-merk vooraan.)
Categorie: Marketing & Verkoop > E-mailing / Notificaties (of "Klantenservice" naargelang de lijst).
Prijs: gratis (de dienst TextMeFlow is gratis tot 50 berichten per maand; daarboven betaalde abonnementen op textmeflow.eu).
Compatibiliteit: PrestaShop 8.0 - 9.x.

## English

**Short description**
Send your customers order updates on WhatsApp, from your own number. Connect in one click.

**Long description**
TextMeFlow lets your shop send WhatsApp messages about orders, from your own WhatsApp number, without a complicated setup.

Setting up takes about two minutes: click "Connect with TextMeFlow", sign in or create a free account, allow the connection, then scan a QR code with WhatsApp. You never copy or paste an API key.

Ready-made messages (English, Dutch, French and German) are switched on for payment received, shipped and delivered. You can switch any order status on or off, edit the text, and use placeholders such as {first_name}, {order_number}, {order_total}, {shop_name} and {tracking_url}.

Customers only receive messages if they tick an opt-in box ("Send me my order updates via WhatsApp") when they register or order. The box is never ticked by default and can be withdrawn in "My account". You can also get a WhatsApp message yourself for every new order.

**Features**
- One-click connection and QR code inside your back office
- Messages on order status changes, per status on or off, editable text
- Opt-in checkbox at registration and checkout (GDPR-friendly)
- Optional message to the shop owner for each new order
- Phone numbers converted to the international format using the address country
- Test message and one-click disconnect
- Errors in the PrestaShop log and as a private note on the order; a failing message never blocks a checkout
- Back office in English, Dutch, French and German

**Screenshots**: see "Afbeeldingen voor Addons" at the bottom of this file.

**FAQ**
- *Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?* No. You link your own WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code, like WhatsApp Web.
- *What does it cost?* The module is free. TextMeFlow is free up to 50 messages per month; larger volumes need a paid TextMeFlow plan.
- *Which customers get messages?* Only customers who ticked the WhatsApp box when registering or ordering.
- *Does it slow down my checkout?* No. Messages are sent after the page has been delivered.
- *How do I stop it?* Click "Disconnect" on the configuration page or uninstall the module; the connection is revoked.

**Privacy and external service**
This module sends the recipient's phone number and the message text to TextMeFlow (EU-hosted, operated by Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria) to deliver the WhatsApp message. Nothing is sent until you connect your account. See https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy and https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms. You remain responsible for obtaining your customers' consent; the module provides the opt-in box.
