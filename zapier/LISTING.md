# Zapier listing text

Name: TextMeFlow (all languages). Zapier requires English; the other languages are for the marketing site and help pages.

## en
- Short: Send WhatsApp messages from your Zaps with your own number.
- Description: TextMeFlow is a WhatsApp messaging service that lets you send WhatsApp messages from your own number through a simple API, hosted in the EU. Link your number by scanning a QR code, then send messages from any Zap. Free up to 50 messages per month.
- Actions: Send WhatsApp Message (text, optional image or file). Searches: Find Message Status.
- Privacy: phone numbers and message text are sent to TextMeFlow (Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria, EU-hosted). https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy, https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms. Only message people who agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from you.
- FAQ: Where do I find my API key? In the portal at textmeflow.eu. Do I need a WhatsApp Business account? No, a normal WhatsApp number is linked via QR. What does it cost? Free up to 50 messages per month.
