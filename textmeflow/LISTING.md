## English

**Name:** TextMeFlow WhatsApp

**Short description (summary):** Send WhatsApp messages from quotes, deliveries and invoices with your own number.

**Long description**

Send order confirmations, delivery updates and invoice links to your customers on WhatsApp, from your own number, without leaving Odoo.

Connecting takes three clicks: press Connect, approve on textmeflow.eu, scan the QR code with WhatsApp. No API key to copy.

Features
- Automatic messages when a quotation is confirmed, a delivery is shipped and a customer invoice is posted. Each can be switched off.
- "Send via WhatsApp" button on contacts, sales orders and invoices (invoices include a link to the customer portal).
- Message templates in English, Dutch, French and German with placeholders; the customer's language is used.
- Consent first: only contacts with "WhatsApp messages allowed" ticked receive messages (date of consent stored).
- Sent in the background: confirming an order or posting an invoice is never slowed down. Every message is logged in the chatter, failed messages can be retried.
- Test message, status check and disconnect button.

External service and privacy: messages are delivered by TextMeFlow (Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria, EU-hosted). The recipient's phone number and the message text are sent to TextMeFlow when a message is sent. TextMeFlow is free up to 50 messages per month; paid plans at https://textmeflow.eu/pricing. Privacy policy: https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy. Terms: https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms. You are responsible for having your customers' consent.

Requires the Sales, Inventory and Invoicing apps.

**FAQ**
- Do I need the WhatsApp Business API? No. You link your own WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code, like WhatsApp Web.
- What does it cost? The module is free; TextMeFlow is free up to 50 messages per month.
- Who receives automatic messages? Only contacts with "WhatsApp messages allowed" ticked.
- Does Odoo slow down? No. Messages are queued and sent by a scheduled action.
- Where is my data? Phone numbers and message texts go to TextMeFlow (EU-hosted). The access token stays in Odoo system parameters.
