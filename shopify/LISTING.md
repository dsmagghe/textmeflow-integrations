## English (en)

**Tagline / subtitle** (max 62):
WhatsApp order updates from your own number, ready in minutes

**Introduction** (max 100):
Send order, shipping and cancellation updates to customers on WhatsApp, from your own number.

**Details** (max 500):
TextMeFlow WhatsApp Notifications sends your customers a WhatsApp message when they place an order, when it ships (with the track and trace link) and when it is cancelled. Messages come from your own WhatsApp number, which you link by scanning a QR code, just like WhatsApp Web. Ready-made texts in English, Dutch, French and German follow the language of your store or customer. Switch each message on or off and edit the text with placeholders such as order number and tracking link.

**Feature list** (max 80 each, max 5):
- Automatic messages for new, shipped and cancelled orders
- Sent from your own WhatsApp number, linked with a QR code
- Ready-made texts in English, Dutch, French and German
- Edit every message, with placeholders for name, order and tracking link
- Option to only message customers who gave SMS permission at checkout

**FAQ**:
- *Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?* No. You link your existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by scanning a QR code. TextMeFlow is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
- *What does it cost?* Installing is free. TextMeFlow is free up to 50 messages per month; larger plans are on textmeflow.eu/pricing.
- *Which customers get a message?* Customers with a phone number in their shipping or billing address. By default only customers who agreed to receive text messages at checkout; you can change this.
- *Can customers stop the messages?* Yes, replying STOP blocks further messages to that number.
- *Which languages?* English, Dutch, French and German, based on the customer's or store's language.
- *What happens when I uninstall?* The connection and access tokens are deleted immediately; remaining store settings are deleted when Shopify sends the store deletion request (48 hours later).

**Privacy and external service**:
This app sends the customer's phone number, first name, order number, order total and tracking link (in the message text) to TextMeFlow, a WhatsApp messaging service hosted in the EU and operated by Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria. Privacy policy: https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy. Terms: https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms. The app itself does not store phone numbers or message texts beyond what is needed to send the message.
