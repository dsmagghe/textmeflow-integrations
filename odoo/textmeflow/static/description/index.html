TextMeFlow WhatsApp

Send order, delivery and invoice messages from your own WhatsApp number.

Connect in three clicks

  1. Open TextMeFlow > Settings and press Connect with TextMeFlow.
  2. Create an account or log in on textmeflow.eu and press Allow. You return to Odoo automatically.
  3. Scan the QR code with WhatsApp (Settings > Linked devices). Ready-made messages are switched on in English, Dutch, French and German.

No API key to copy, nothing technical to configure.

What it does

  • Automatic messages when a quotation is confirmed, a delivery is shipped and an invoice is posted (each can be switched off).
  • A Send via WhatsApp button on contacts, sales orders and customer invoices. Invoices include a link to the customer portal.
  • Editable message templates with placeholders such as {first_name}, {order_number} and {portal_url}, in English, Dutch, French and German. The customer's language is used.
  • Privacy by design: only contacts with the WhatsApp messages allowed box ticked receive messages.
  • Messages are sent in the background, so confirming an order or posting an invoice is never slowed down. Every message is logged in the chatter.
  • Test button, status check and a Disconnect button.

Price and external service

The module is free. Messages are delivered by the external service TextMeFlow, which is free for up to 50 messages per month; paid plans are listed at textmeflow.eu/pricing.

Data sent to the service: the recipient's phone number and the message text, only when a message is sent. The service is operated by Fades Management EOOD (Bulgaria) and hosted in the EU. See the privacy policy and the terms.

You are responsible for having your customers' consent to WhatsApp messages. The module only sends to contacts where consent is recorded.

Compatible with Odoo 17 and 18 Community and Enterprise. Requires the Sales, Inventory and Invoicing apps.
