# Make listing text

Name: TextMeFlow

## en
- Short: Send WhatsApp messages from your own number in Make scenarios.
- Long: TextMeFlow is a WhatsApp messaging service with an EU-hosted API. Link your own WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code, then send messages, check their status and react to replies in your scenarios. Free up to 50 messages per month.
- Modules: Send a WhatsApp Message, Get a Message, Watch Incoming Messages.
- Privacy / external service: phone numbers and message text are sent to TextMeFlow (Fades Management EOOD, Bulgaria, EU-hosted). https://textmeflow.eu/legal/privacy, https://textmeflow.eu/legal/terms. Only message people who agreed to receive WhatsApp messages.
- FAQ: API key = textmeflow.eu portal. No WhatsApp Business account needed. Free up to 50 messages per month.
